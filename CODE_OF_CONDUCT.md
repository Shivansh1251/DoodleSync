# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

DoodleSync aims to be a welcoming, respectful place for everyone. We want project spaces where people can share ideas, learn, and collaborate without harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, respectful, and constructive.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and give feedback thoughtfully.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not on people.
- Respect others’ privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, intimidation, unwelcome sexual attention, doxxing, and publishing someone else’s private information are not acceptable. Deliberately disrupting project discussions or targeting people is also not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in DoodleSync repositories, issues, pull requests, and project-related community spaces. Project maintainers may remove content or take other action they consider appropriate, including restricting participation.

Report a concern privately to the project maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/Shivansh1251). Please include enough detail to help review what happened. Reports will be handled as discreetly as possible.
